Does KindMoment send messages automatically?

No. KindMoment prepares the text and opens your selected messaging app. You review the message and initiate sending. A handoff does not guarantee delivery by another platform.

Why did my reminder make no sound?

Check that notifications and sounds are allowed for KindMoment in iOS Settings. Silent Mode, Focus and notification summaries can also affect sound or vibration.

How do I restore KindMoment Premium?

Open KindMoment Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription.

What happens after I cancel?

Cancellation prevents the next renewal. Apple normally keeps access active until the end of the current paid period.

How do I delete my local data?

Open Settings, then Privacy Center, and choose the option to delete local app data. Removing the app also removes its local data from that device.
